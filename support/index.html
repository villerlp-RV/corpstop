Getting started

  1. Sign in at app.linetally.com.
  2. On Portfolio, click Add system for each water system you run, with its service-line inventory totals. Or click Load example data to look around first, and Remove example data when you're done.
  3. Open Company and add your logo, business name and address so they appear on every report.
  4. Open Team, create a team for your company, and share the invite code with office staff and field techs.

Common questions

How do I invite someone to my team?

On the Team page, copy the invite code and send it to them. They sign in, open Team, enter the code under "Join a team". Only the team owner sees the code and can make a new one, which stops the old code working.

How do I send a field tech to check service lines?

Open a system, tick the service lines in the list, choose the person under "Assign to" and click Assign. They appear on the tech's Field work page under Mine.

Does it work without cell signal?

Yes, once the app has been opened with signal. Visits recorded with no signal are saved on the phone and show under "Waiting to upload"; they upload automatically when signal returns, or tap Upload now. Don't clear the browser's data or sign out of the phone's browser while visits are waiting.

The camera doesn't open

Your browser needs permission to use the camera. Allow it when asked, or turn it on in the browser's site settings for app.linetally.com. If the in-app camera still won't open, LineTally opens your phone's own camera instead, and you can always use "From library".

How do I make a report?

Click Generate report on Portfolio for a summary of every system, or on a system page for that system's full compliance report. Reports download as PDF with your company letterhead from the Company page.

How do I export for my state?

Open State reports, pick the state, and use Copy CSV or Download CSV. The layout is generic; check your state's required template before submitting.

I don't see my latest changes on another device

Changes sync within seconds. If a device was asleep or offline, bring the app to the front or refresh the page once.

How do I delete my data or account?

You can delete systems and service lines in the app. To delete your account or your team's data entirely, email support@linetally.com. See the Privacy Policy.
